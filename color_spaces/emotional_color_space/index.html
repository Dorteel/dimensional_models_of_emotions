Geometry and interpretation

The six boundary hues are red (0°), yellow (60°), green (120°), cyan (180°), blue (240°), and magenta (300°). Intermediate hues interpolate along straight hexagon edges. Saturation scales from the grayscale axis to the boundary; value scales the entire horizontal hexagon and sets its height. At V = 0 all coordinates collapse to black.

The translucent horizontal slice marks V. The dark inner hexagon marks S at that height; the colored ray marks H. The colored marker sits at their intersection. Hue is a sector coordinate, so its Euclidean polar angle differs slightly between corners. This HSV embedding is not the RGB cube's perpendicular plane section, and it is not perceptually uniform.

Emotion names are assigned only by you. Energy and love / positive orientation are interpretations under exploration, not measurements or psychological diagnoses. At S = 0 hue does not change the gray color; at V = 0 neither hue nor saturation changes black.
